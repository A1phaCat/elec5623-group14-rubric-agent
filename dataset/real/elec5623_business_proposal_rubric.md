# ELEC5623 Group Business Proposal — Canvas marking rubric (10 marks)

Source: Canvas assignment "Business Proposal" rubric, course 74325, read 13 Sep 2026. Level text is verbatim; IDs are ours.

CRITERION: Problem, Background and Motivation
ID: R1
MAX: 1.5
GRANULARITY: 0.5
1.5: The problem is specific, meaningful and well scoped; background is supported by credible evidence; the motivation and identified gap are compelling; the need for GenAI is clearly and convincingly justified.
1: The problem and motivation are clear and relevant; background is generally well supported; the role of GenAI is appropriately justified, with only minor gaps in evidence or argument.
0.5: The problem is vague, weakly motivated or poorly supported; background is superficial; the need for GenAI is unclear or appears incidental to the proposed product.
0: The criterion is not addressed, or the submitted material provides no meaningful evidence of the problem, background or motivation.

CRITERION: Requirements Engineering and Project Definition
ID: R2
MAX: 1.5
GRANULARITY: 0.5
1.5: Target users and stakeholders are clearly identified; project scope and boundaries are precise; use cases are appropriate; functional and non-functional requirements are coherent, measurable and testable.
1: Users, scope, use cases and requirements are mostly well defined; requirements are generally testable and aligned with the proposed system, with minor omissions or ambiguities.
0.5: Requirements are incomplete, inconsistent or largely descriptive rather than testable; users, stakeholders or project scope are poorly defined.
0: No meaningful requirements engineering, scope definition, stakeholder analysis or use cases are provided.

CRITERION: Proposed Product, Methodology and Technical Design
ID: R3
MAX: 2
GRANULARITY: 0.5
2: The proposed solution is technically credible and clearly defined; the role of GenAI is explicit and necessary; methodology, architecture and workflow are coherent and sufficiently detailed; components and integrations form a feasible semester-scale system.
1.5: The technical approach is sound and mostly well explained; GenAI plays a meaningful role; architecture and workflow are appropriate, although some implementation details or technical relationships require refinement.
1: A plausible technical solution is proposed, but the methodology, architecture or workflow lacks detail; the GenAI component is present but its exact role or integration is only partially explained.
0.5: The technical design is vague, inconsistent or unrealistic; major components or workflow are missing; GenAI is used superficially or without a clear technical justification.
0: No meaningful proposed methodology, architecture, workflow or technical solution is provided.

CRITERION: Product/Business Analysis and Evaluation Plan
ID: R4
MAX: 2
GRANULARITY: 0.5
2: The target users and value proposition are compelling; relevant alternatives or competitors are critically considered; evaluation includes clear success criteria, appropriate baselines, meaningful metrics and credible evidence collection.
1.5: Product value and relevant alternatives are clearly discussed; the proposed evaluation is generally appropriate and includes useful metrics and success criteria, with minor gaps.
1: Basic product value and evaluation ideas are provided, but competitor/alternative analysis is limited; metrics or baselines are incomplete or only partially connected to project objectives.
0.5: Product value is poorly justified; little meaningful analysis of alternatives is provided; evaluation relies mainly on subjective demonstrations or vague success measures.
0: No meaningful product/business analysis or evaluation plan is provided.

CRITERION: Feasibility, Risk, Semester Plan and Team Roles
ID: R5
MAX: 1.5
GRANULARITY: 0.5
1.5: Scope is realistic and appropriately constrained; major technical and project risks are clearly identified with credible mitigation strategies; milestones are achievable and team responsibilities are clearly allocated.
1: A basic semester plan and allocation of responsibilities are provided; feasibility is plausible but scope, risks or milestones require further refinement.
0.5: The project appears overly ambitious, poorly scoped or insufficiently planned; major risks are ignored; milestones or team responsibilities are unclear.
0: No meaningful evidence of feasibility analysis, risk management, semester planning or team responsibilities is provided.

CRITERION: Communication, Professionalism and References
ID: R6
MAX: 1.5
GRANULARITY: 0.5
1.5: The proposal is clear, concise, coherent and professionally presented; figures and tables are effective where appropriate; external claims are supported by relevant and credible sources with consistent referencing.
1: The proposal is understandable but contains organisational, writing or presentation issues; references are present but may be limited, inconsistently formatted or weakly connected to claims.
0.5: The proposal is difficult to follow or poorly presented; writing contains substantial clarity issues; references are weak, inappropriate or inconsistently used.
0: The submission lacks an acceptable professional presentation and/or provides no meaningful referencing.
